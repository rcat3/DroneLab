# Deprecated Hardware

Designs that are no longer supported, kept for anyone who wants to build them or bring them back.  They aren't kept up to date with the firmware or the other housings, and the firmware may no longer drive what's in them.  Each folder's README says why it was dropped, what replaces it, and where to find the last firmware that worked with it.

| Design | For | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| [Speaker housing](waveshare_esp32s3_touch_lcd_1_46_speaker_housing/) | Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.46, with two speakers, their amplifiers and the J9 header adapter PCB | The [ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75](../boards/waveshare_esp32s3_touch_amoled_1_75/) and its speaker housing.  The 1.46" board itself is still supported, in its [simple housing](../boards/waveshare_esp32s3_touch_lcd_1_46/). |

Each design keeps its own license, given in its README.
