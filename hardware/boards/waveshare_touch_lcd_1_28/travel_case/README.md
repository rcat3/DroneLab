# Travel Case for the 1.28" Housing

A hinged case that carries the [1.28" housing](../) with the board in it, so the display is protected in a bag.

## Files

- `travel_case_1_28.stl` - All five parts laid out to print: the bottom, the lid, the hinge, the clasp and the hinge pin.  The layout is 109 x 159 mm.

Inside, the case has a round pocket 57.6 mm across and 20.5 mm deep, which fits the housing with about 0.3 mm to spare around it.  It's the 1.46" housing's travel case scaled up evenly to 109.84%, so the pocket fits the 1.28" housing's 57 mm body, with the lid's pocket made 3 mm shallower so the housing doesn't rattle.  Scaling every part by the same amount keeps the hinge, the clasp and the pin working together.  The holes for the filament the hinge and clasp turn on were then put back to the 1.46" case's 2.1 mm, as scaling had made them 2.3 mm, too loose to hold 1.75 mm filament.  The exception is the clasp's hole, which runs the clasp's whole 26 mm length and prints standing up, so it comes out smaller and the filament won't go all the way through at 2.1 mm.  It's 2.4 mm, and the lid's holes either side of it still hold the filament.  The bar the clasp hooks onto is 4.6 mm across at its ends, where they sit in the bottom's 4.67 mm holes, so it can't slide out, and as scaled, 4.23 mm, in the middle, where the clasp goes round it.  The housing is 19.6 mm tall with either board, so the case fits either one.  A groove 2.2 mm deep runs around the bottom's pocket for the housing's switch lever, which stands out of its side.  It covers where the lever ends up with the housing either way up, and it's at any angle, so the housing can go in turned any way.  Its top slopes at 45 degrees, so it prints without supports.
